How an experienced analyst handles this attack

  • Work out the pattern before you act. Micro-authorisations, one BIN range, datacenter ASNs and high velocity point to a rate limit plus a block on that range and those ASNs. Blocking everything in a panic swaps a fraud loss for a bigger revenue loss.
  • Do not chase IP addresses, since a bot rotates them in seconds. Aim at the BIN range and the velocity, which it cannot rotate away from.
  • Contain the attack first and then report it. A short note on what happened, what you did and what you recommend earns trust. Silence blindsides leadership, and paging everyone teaches people to tune you out.
