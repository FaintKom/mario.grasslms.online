FinTechCard SDR mini-OS

A new SDR sits down to seven apps, and the job moves between them all day: Outreach to dial, Gong to review the call, Salesforce to log the next step, Calendar to book it, LinkedIn to research the account, Phone for the live conversation, Slack for the team.

Invented brief. Packaged as SCORM 1.2, so it runs in any LMS or on its own. Click an icon on the right to open an app.
