Job aid: briefs, checks and fix requests for three kinds of draft

Keep this page next to the model. The columns are the three moves that stay with you, and the rows are the drafts they apply to.

DraftPut in the briefCheck before a colleague sees itWord the fix request
Lesson plan
  • Learner: who they are and what they can already do
  • Objective: what they do at the end, in words you could watch
  • Limits: minutes, room, tools
  • Source: the document, pasted in, with "use nothing else"
  • Format: a table with minutes per row and the total under it
  • Objective: could you watch a learner do it?
  • Practice: does each activity make learners do what the objective names?
  • Facts: can you point to the source line for every number?
  • Fit: do the minutes add up, with the room and tools you have?
 "Row 5: replace the quiz with three tickets agents decide alone, 5 minutes. Row 3: the limit is €150, as in the notes. Change nothing else."
Slide deck
  • The approved plan as the only source
  • Pass 1: one line per slide, each with its plan row
  • Pass 2: title, up to four points, trainer's note, with the template's limits stated
  • Pass 3: fields into the template, no design changes
  • Each slide points to a plan row, and no row is left without a slide
  • Each slide fits the limits of the template
  • Every figure matches the plan
  • Nothing overflows after pass 3
 "Slides 2 and 6: cut, no plan row. Slide 3: keep the first four points and move the rest to the slide for row 4. Slide 4: the limit is €150. Add a slide for row 5."
SCORM exercise
  • Decision: what the learner decides and sees
  • Cases: each with its answer and rule, from the source
  • Feedback: the rule that applies, never a bare verdict
  • States: start, each case, result, second attempt
  • Report: score, pass mark, when it is sent
  • Tests: what you will run yourself
  • Play it with a wrong answer on every case
  • Each correct answer matches the source
  • Feedback names the rule
  • The log shows Initialize, the score, the status, Commit and Finish
  • Every button works from the keyboard
 "Ticket 3: the correct answer is Escalate, rule 4. Feedback must name the rule, as in part 3 of the spec. On the result screen send score.raw and set the status to passed at 3 or more. Change nothing else."
