How to describe a picture for Perchance

Perchance is a website that draws a picture from the words you type, and the more clearly you describe what you want, the closer the picture gets to it. This workshop explains the controls on the page and then helps you build a description.

Please open Perchance with a teacher, because the site is open to everyone and has a public gallery and a chat.

Good to know

    Five styles to start with

    The 🎨 Art Style menu has dozens of styles, and these five are a good place to begin.

    Leaving things out

    Perchance itself advises against writing "no blue", because the word blue stays in your description and the picture pays attention to it. Describe the picture you do want instead.
