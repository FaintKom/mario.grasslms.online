## Scope
Portfolio shell: home (`index.html`), demo landings (`pipeline/mini-cases/index.html`, `pipeline/engagement/index.html`), compliance design map, kids catalogue. Visitor mode: Experience. Kids activities and the GrassLMS carousel keep their LMS look.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A hands-on exhibit hall. Every simulation is an exhibit on a coloured plinth with a placard that says, in two plain paragraphs, what you do in it and why it is built that way. It refuses the category default of a hero headline over a grid of equal case cards.
OWN-WORLD: GrassLMS palette only. Paper ground (#fbfcf7), ink (#0d150d) text and 3px ink rules, plinths in lagoon-600, green-700, clay-600, ink-900, lagoon-800, green-500, sun-400; sun-400 placard for the featured exhibit. Archivo (wide, 850) for headings, Manrope for text. No mono labels, no eyebrows, no chips, no uppercase micro-captions; facts live in sentences.
STORY: A hiring manager in EdTech or L&D learns who Mario is in one sentence, plays a working simulation on the first screen, reads why it is designed that way, then scans the other exhibits, shipped client programs, tools, the LMS and the children's work before contacting him.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the full name left and plain underlined CV and LinkedIn links right. First-person heading "I design courses and build the simulations inside them" left, a two-sentence intro right. Below, the featured exhibit: the fraud review queue running live in a lagoon plinth (60% width), the sun placard beside it with heading, two paragraphs and "Open the review queue".
FORM: Science-centre exhibit placards (Exploratorium try-this / what's-going-on), ranked first on my list; seed key 4992ed70 (the user chose the pick card).
SIGNATURE: a floor plan fixed to the bottom of the viewport; a yellow "you are here" block slides to the part of the page in view and every item jumps to its room.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
